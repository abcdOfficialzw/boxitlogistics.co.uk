FAQs | Boxit Logistics House and Office Removals in Walsall and Birmingham

Boxit Logistics is a professional removals and logistics company based in Walsall Wood, serving customers across the West Midlands and nationwide. Below are answers to the questions we are asked most often about house moves, office relocations, furniture delivery, storage, service areas, quotes, and booking.

House moving services

How much does a house move cost?

The cost depends on property size, distance between addresses, access restrictions, and whether furniture dismantling or additional services are required.

To receive an accurate quote, send the collection and delivery postcodes, property size, preferred date, and any details of large or specialist items.

Do you provide long-distance house removals?

Yes. We carry out both local house removals in Walsall and long-distance moves across the UK, including Birmingham and London. The service includes secure loading, protective handling, reliable scheduling, and careful unloading.

Do you dismantle and reassemble furniture during a move?

Yes. We offer professional furniture dismantling and reassembly for items such as beds, wardrobes, desks, and dining tables.

Can you help with packing for my house move?

We can provide advice on preparing and packing ahead of moving day. If you require more support, contact us to discuss the details.

Office moving services

Do you offer office removals?

Yes. We provide office removals in Walsall and across the wider West Midlands for small businesses, growing companies, industrial units, and commercial premises.

How long does an office move usually take?

Small office moves within the local area can often be completed in one day. Larger relocations take longer depending on the volume of equipment, IT systems, access conditions, and distance.

Can you safely move office furniture and equipment?

Yes. We use professional equipment and safe handling procedures to protect desks, filing systems, and business equipment during transport.

Furniture delivery and specialist transport

Do you offer furniture delivery services?

Yes. Furniture delivery is available in Walsall and surrounding areas for store collections, marketplace purchases, single large items, and urgent deliveries.

Do you handle urgent or short-notice removals?

Yes, subject to availability. If you need a last-minute moving company, contact us as soon as possible and we will confirm availability.

Do you move single items rather than full house moves?

Yes. We can help with flexible single-item transport such as sofas, appliances, and wardrobes.

Storage solutions

Do you offer short-term storage?

Yes. We offer short-term storage solutions for customers who need temporary space during a move, renovation, or property transition.

Can I combine storage with a house move?

Yes. Many customers combine removals and storage when moving dates do not align. We can coordinate collection, storage, and re-delivery.

Areas we cover and practical information

What areas do you serve?

Boxit Logistics is based in Walsall Wood and serves Birmingham, Wolverhampton, the wider West Midlands, and long-distance routes across the UK to places such as London.

Do you offer free quotes for removals?

Yes. All quotes are free and without obligation. The quickest way to enquire is through our contact form, email, or WhatsApp/SMS.

How do I book a move with Boxit Logistics?

Send us your move details, receive your personalised quote, confirm your date, and we complete the move as agreed.

Are you insured for house and office removals?

We use safe handling procedures and professional equipment to safeguard your belongings. Contact us directly if you require insurance detail confirmation.
