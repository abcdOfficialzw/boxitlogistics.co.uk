Frequently Asked Questions

How much does CCTV installation cost?

The cost depends on the size of your property, the number of cameras required and the specification of the system. We'll provide a clear, no-obligation quotation before any work begins.

Can I view my CCTV cameras on my phone?

Many modern CCTV systems support secure remote viewing through dedicated smartphone applications, allowing you to monitor your property from almost anywhere with an internet connection.

How many security cameras do I need?

Every property is different. During our consultation, we'll recommend the number and placement of cameras needed to provide effective coverage without unnecessary equipment.

Can CCTV cameras be installed indoors and outdoors?

Yes. We install cameras suitable for both internal and external locations, ensuring each one is positioned for maximum effectiveness.

Are there any rules for installing CCTV at home?

If your cameras capture images beyond the boundary of your property, you may need to comply with data protection legislation. The Information Commissioner's Office provides useful guidance for domestic CCTV users at ICO.

Will my CCTV record at night?

Many modern CCTV systems include infrared night vision or low-light technology, allowing them to continue recording clear footage after dark.
