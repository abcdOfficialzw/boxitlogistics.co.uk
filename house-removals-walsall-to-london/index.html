Furniture dismantling and assembly included

Beds, wardrobes, tables, desks, and other bulky items can be dismantled before transport and reassembled at your London property. This reduces the risk of damage and helps with narrow access routes.

Practical London move considerations

Parking controls, loading restrictions, building management rules, lift access, and stair access can all affect a London move. We recommend confirming these details in advance so moving day runs efficiently.

What to confirm before moving day

  • Parking availability at the delivery address
  • Any access restrictions or time slot rules
  • Lift or stair access details
  • Building management permissions where required
