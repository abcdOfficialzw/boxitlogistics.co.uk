Can You Hire a Man With a Van for One Item?

5 August 2026 Boxit Logistics Team

Whether you've bought a sofa from Facebook Marketplace, need to collect a wardrobe from a furniture shop, or want to move a washing machine to another property, you might be wondering if hiring a man with a van for just one item is possible.

The simple answer is yes.

Many professional removals companies offer single-item collections and deliveries, making them an ideal option when you don't need a full house move. Instead of hiring a van yourself, trying to lift heavy furniture with friends, or worrying about whether everything will fit, you can leave the hard work to experienced movers.

That said, not every man with a van service offers the same level of care. If you're moving something valuable, bulky or awkward, it's worth knowing what to look for before booking.

When Does It Make Sense to Hire a Man With a Van?

Hiring a man with a van isn't just for people moving house.

Single-item deliveries are a popular choice for anyone who needs help transporting something too large or heavy for a standard car.

Common examples include:

  • Sofas and armchairs
  • Beds and mattresses
  • Wardrobes
  • Dining tables
  • Washing machines and fridge freezers
  • Office desks and chairs
  • Gym equipment
  • Furniture bought online or in-store

Whether you're buying second-hand furniture, helping a family member move, or furnishing a rental property, a professional collection service can save time, effort and unnecessary stress.

It's Not Just About Lifting Something Heavy

One of the biggest misconceptions is that moving furniture is simply about strength.

In reality, the biggest challenge is usually getting the item out of one property and into another without causing damage.

Think about the obstacles that often get overlooked:

Narrow hallways Tight staircases Sharp corners Low ceilings Apartment blocks Limited parking Heavy fire doors

A large sofa might not be especially heavy, but manoeuvring it around awkward corners without damaging walls or the furniture itself takes experience.

Professional movers do this every day. They understand how to position bulky items, protect them during transport, and move them safely through difficult spaces.

Is a Man With a Van Always the Best Option?

Not necessarily.

For a lightweight item travelling a short distance, a basic man with a van service may be perfectly suitable.

However, if you're moving something that's:

  • particularly valuable
  • unusually heavy
  • antique or fragile
  • travelling a long distance
  • difficult to access
  • likely to need dismantling

then using a professional removals company is often the better choice.

You're not just paying someone to drive a van.

You're paying for experience, safe lifting techniques, protective equipment and the reassurance that your belongings will be handled with care throughout the journey.

Questions Worth Asking Before You Book

Before choosing a company, it's worth asking a few simple questions.

For example:

  • Is my item insured during transport?
  • Will two people attend if the furniture is heavy?
  • Do you use protective blankets and securing straps?
  • Can you dismantle furniture if needed?
  • Will you carry the item into the room I want it placed?
  • Do you offer long-distance deliveries?

A professional company should be happy to answer these questions before confirming your booking.

Buying Furniture Online? You're Not Alone

Buying furniture online has become increasingly common.

Whether it's a bargain on Facebook Marketplace, a wardrobe from IKEA or a dining table found on eBay, one question always follows:

How do I get it home?

Hiring a man with a van is often the easiest solution.

Many professional transport companies regularly collect furniture from:

Facebook Marketplace eBay Gumtree IKEA Dunelm Local furniture retailers Storage facilities

Rather than borrowing a van or trying to squeeze everything into your own car, the furniture can be collected and delivered directly to your home.

Don't Forget About Furniture Dismantling

Sometimes the biggest challenge isn't transporting the furniture.

It's getting it through the front door.

Large wardrobes, bed frames, dining tables and office furniture often need dismantling before they can be moved safely.

Choosing a company that also provides Furniture Assembly & Dismantling means your furniture can be carefully taken apart, transported securely and reassembled at its new location, helping to reduce the risk of damage and saving you valuable time.

A Few Things You Can Do Before Collection Day

Preparing your furniture before collection helps everything run more smoothly.

Before the driver arrives, it's worth:

  • Measuring doorways, hallways and staircases.
  • Emptying drawers and cupboards.
  • Disconnecting appliances safely.
  • Removing loose shelves where possible.
  • Letting the company know about restricted access or parking.
  • Mentioning if the item is particularly valuable or fragile.

Providing accurate information beforehand allows the company to bring the right vehicle and equipment for the job.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you hire a man with a van for one item?

Yes. Many removals companies offer single-item collections for furniture, appliances and other bulky belongings.

Will they help lift the furniture?

Most professional services include loading and unloading, although it's always worth checking exactly what's included when booking.

Can a man with a van collect furniture from Facebook Marketplace or IKEA?

Yes. Collecting furniture from online marketplaces and retailers is one of the most common reasons people hire this type of service.

What if my furniture won't fit through the door?

Many removals companies also offer furniture dismantling and reassembly, allowing larger items to be moved safely.

Is a removals company better than a standard man with a van?

It depends on what you're moving. For valuable, heavy or awkward furniture, a professional removals company often provides additional peace of mind through specialist equipment, experienced movers and services such as furniture assembly, dismantling and long-distance delivery.

Moving One Item Doesn't Have to Be Difficult

Hiring a man with a van for one item is often the simplest way to move bulky furniture safely and efficiently.

The important thing is choosing a provider that offers more than just transport. Experience, careful handling and the right equipment can make a significant difference, particularly when moving valuable or awkward items.
